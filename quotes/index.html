    At the edge of the village, a narrow path disappeared into the pines.
    Mira followed it each evening, carrying a notebook and a small brass key.
    She had found the key beneath a loose floorboard in her grandfather’s study.
    No lock in the house seemed to fit it.
    One rainy afternoon, she noticed a stone door hidden behind the roots.
    The key turned with a quiet click, and the door opened into a warm, bright room.
    Inside stood shelves of journals, each marked with a year and a name.
    The newest journal had her own name on its cover.
    She opened it and found a blank page waiting for the first sentence.
    Mira sat by the window and began to write.
